# B2B Sales Playbook — HSN Warrior Apparels

Domain knowledge for closing gym/club clients and teaching the user. Sources: B2B apparel-sales research + live client behavior in this pipeline.

## Prioritization
- **Chains and franchises first.** One closed chain = every location's orders (a Spanish boutique boxing chain in the pipeline runs 9+ locations). Track multi-location gyms in the sheet's analysis column and pitch centralized/bulk ordering.
- **Distributors and resellers are bigger deals than gyms** — they reorder continuously. Flag any lead that SELLS gear, not just trains people, for a dedicated pitch.
- **Reply speed wins deals:** most B2B orders go to whoever answers first. When a client replies, draft instantly so the user can approve within minutes, not hours.

## What gym owners buy first
- Pads + gloves dominate first orders (confirmed by live replies). Lead with protection gear and team wear, not uniforms.
- Low MOQ is the differentiator vs big manufacturers who force 100+ unit orders — keep "club-friendly minimums" in every message; it removes the owner's fear of overstocking.

## Touch sequence
- Email + IG follow + IG DM = 3 touches on day one (standard).
- B2B buyers typically need 5–7 touches before replying: after 4–5 days of silence, ONE short follow-up ("checking the prices reached you — happy to quote quantities") roughly doubles response rates. One bump per silence window, never more.
- When a client replies, the conversation is the user's — drafts only (SKILL.md rule 1).

## Samples
- "Free sample" offers are banned (user removed them). The industry-standard alternative that filters serious buyers: a paid sample at factory price — "order 1 unit to test quality before bulk." Propose only with the user's approval.

## Pricing conversations
- Send the full price list when asked; never drip individual prices unprompted (the EUR list lives in SKILL.md).
- Close by asking for quantities + their logo → quote/mockup → order. That ask is the commitment step.
- "Logo included" and "tax-free shipping" are the two included perks — state them with every price list.

## Market behavior observed in this pipeline
- PT/ES gyms expose WA numbers most often; IT gyms reply in Italian (Google Translate approach approved); BE/AT/CH contacts are mostly email-only; Nordic sites rarely list WA and are often unreachable from the VPS.
