The chain system

The system

Why the chain matters
to buyers

Rather than one tailor assembling a whole garment, the chain passes each piece through specialists — the person who collars a gi collars hundreds a week. That repetition is where Sialkot's quality reputation comes from, and it's why our stitching stays consistent from unit 30 to unit 3,000.

  • Repetition builds consistency at any order size
  • Each stage inspected before the next begins
  • Masters supervise the full chain end-to-end
  • Same line runs your sample and your bulk

Questions

What buyers ask first

What's your minimum order?
Standard minimums start at 30 units per style, mixed sizes. For multi-category programs we can structure minimums across the range rather than per style.
How much does a sample cost?
Pre-production samples are charged at a nominal rate and credited back against your bulk order. Sampling happens on the same line as production, so what you approve is what ships.
What are your lead times?
Typically 2–3 weeks for sampling and 3–5 weeks for bulk production, depending on quantity and decoration. Shipping adds 5–7 days by air, 25–40 days by sea.
Can you handle our tech pack?
Yes — send measurements, materials, construction notes and artwork. If you don't have a tech pack yet, a reference garment or clear photos with notes is enough to start.
Do you ship worldwide?
We export globally from Sialkot with full documentation. Air and sea both available; we can ship to your freight forwarder or door-to-door where offered.
Which payment terms do you accept?
Standard terms are T/T — typically 50% with order confirmation and 50% before shipment, after inspection. We also work with L/C for larger programs.
