Who we are

A Sialkot house
built on the chain

HSN Warrior Apparels manufactures martial arts equipment and custom sportswear for brands, academies and distributors worldwide — from the Sialkot Industrial Estate, Pakistan.

Our story

Fifteen years of gear
that earns its keep

HSN Warrior Apparels was established in 2011 in Sialkot — the city that has manufactured the world's sporting goods for over a century. We began with martial arts uniforms and fightwear, and grew by doing one thing well: building exactly what the customer specified, and shipping it on time.

Today we produce eight product categories — all on our own production line under experienced masters. Our customers rebrand our output and sell it worldwide; their reputation rides on our stitching, and we build like it.

We're a B2B house by design. Our line exists for people who need consistent quality at wholesale, run after run.

Sialkot

Made where the world's gear is made

Sialkot, Pakistan produces a remarkable share of the world's sports goods — from footballs stitched for World Cups to the gloves and gis used in gyms on every continent. Manufacturing here isn't an industry; it's a century-old craft ecosystem, and our unit is part of it.
