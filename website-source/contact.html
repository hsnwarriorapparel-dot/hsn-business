Before you write

A few quick answers

Do you work with new brands?
Yes — plenty of our customers started with one small order. Low minimums exist exactly so new labels can launch without container-scale risk.
Can I get a sample before committing?
Always. Every program starts with a pre-production sample made on the bulk line. You approve it before we cut a single metre of bulk fabric.
Can you match an existing garment we sell?
Send us the reference piece — we'll reverse the pattern, fabric and construction, then sample it for your approval before quoting bulk.
How do we communicate during production?
Email or WhatsApp, directly with the team running your order. Production photos at cutting, stitching and packing stages are available on request.
